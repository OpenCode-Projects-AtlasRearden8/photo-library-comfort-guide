# The Monthly Triage Hour

This is the spine of the whole guide: one hour, once a month, that keeps your camera roll from ever becoming a horror film again. It is not deep clean. It is not forever. It is triage — the quick, kind sorting that keeps everything manageable, the way [triage](glossary.md#triage) works in an emergency room: fast decisions, most important things first, no heroics.

Ten months from now, this hour is the difference between a roll you love and a roll you fear. It is the same hour every time. You will wind up doing it half-asleep, which is the entire point.

## Before you start

Do these once, tonight, so the hour itself is friction-free:

- Phone charged above 50 percent
- Calendar recurring event created, titled "photo hour", with a paper cup emoji or snooze-proof note in the title
- The [Monthly Worksheet](../worksheet/triage-worksheet.md) printed twice, one in your wallet or junk drawer
- One playlist and one beverage of choice

Bring your phone's own notes app open. You will take two lines of notes, tops.

## The hour ahead

Here is the whole session. Print-speed glance-version lives on the worksheet; the real descriptions live below:

| Clock | Block | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| 0–10 min | Sweep | Delete the obvious: screenshots older than 30 days, pocket shots, brighter-blur accidents |
| 10–30 min | Duplicates | Exact copies and near-dupes, using chapter two's rules |
| 30–45 min | Albums | Sort the month's keepers into your album set |
| 45–55 min | Favorites | Star anything you love; set one as a print or gift candidate |
| 55–60 min | Close-out | Two-line note, count the keepers, schedule anything owed |

If your backlog is large, your first session may need to be three payments of 20 minutes across three evenings instead of one hour. The [low-energy path](00-start-here.md#a-note-on-energy-and-focus) is always valid. The rhythm matters more than the terms of delivery.

## Block one — the sweep, minutes 0–10

Scroll your camera roll from earliest to latest and delete without mercy things that cannot be precious:

- Screenshots of food delivery apps, discount codes, price comparisons, whiteboards, and grocery lists you have already shopped from
- [Pocket shots](glossary.md#pocket-shot) — the mysterious photos of your pocket, ceiling, steering wheel, shoe, or the inside of your bag
- Blurry accidents, photos of your thumb, and the three nearly identical black-and-amber photos of your phone struggling to focus on a lamp

Rule of the block: if you hesitate for more than two seconds, **it stays**. The test is not "is this garbage?" The test is "is this garbage beyond a shadow of a doubt?" Hesitation means keep. It will return next month, when you will have had a full month of evidence about whether the photo matters.

## Block two — duplicates, minutes 10–30

Open the phone's own duplicate tooling if available (IPhones have had a built-in Duplicates album since 2023; many Android gallery and storage tools offer a cleaner). Where you want something stronger, your sibling project [photo-dedupe-lite-cli](glossary.md#photo-dedupe-lite-cli) handles exact copies and, cautiously, near-dupes on your own computer.

Details, tactics, and tie-breaker rules live in [Duplicates, Decoded](02-duplicates-decoded.md). This block is just: run the tool, review its suggestions, approve the boring ones, argue with the near-dupes.

## Block three — albums, minutes 30–45

Take the keepers from this month and put them into your small set of albums. The full reasoning lives in [Albums You'll Actually Keep](03-albums-youll-actually-keep.md), but the block's own rule is simple: **there are only six albums plus an archive, and you already know which album a photo belongs in within three seconds.** If it takes longer, leave it in the roll. The camera roll itself is album zero — sorted by time, which is a perfectly good retrieval system most of the time.

## Block four — favorites, minutes 45–55

Star anything that makes you smile on the way past. Do not overthink it; favoriting is cheap and idempotent. This block creates tomorrow's joy: a print-worthy pile, plus the gift ideas that fill [Print, Books, and Gifts](07-print-books-and-gifts.md).

## Block five — the close-out, minutes 55–60

Two lines in your notes app:

```text
Photo hour, [month]: deleted N, albums added: [names], favorites: N
Owed: [anything I skipped on purpose]
```

Then set a bookmark for anything owed. Guilt stays out; facts stay in. That note is your [worksheet](../worksheet/triage-worksheet.md) log too, if you printed one.

## If you fall behind

You will, at least once. Everyone does. The reset ritual is deliberately small:

1. Do not scroll your whole backlog. That's the trap.
2. Do a 15-minute version of the sweep block only, newest to oldest, one screen at a time.
3. Resume the normal hour next month. The roll shrinks two blocks per session after that.

A photo hour missed is a photo hour in escrow. Nothing compounds in the meantime; photos are not people waiting on hold. The roll waits patiently, without judgment, for you to come back.
